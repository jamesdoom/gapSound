# Publisher feed policy review

Reviewed **November 25, 2026** for GapSound's current local, single-user, personal and non-commercial RSS/CVE reader. This records published evidence and engineering decisions, not legal clearance for every use. A reachable feed or robots allowance is not a copyright license. No publisher was contacted, no paid access was added and no public redistribution was enabled.

The 15-minute defaults and CVE spacing are application choices, not publisher promises or claimed contractual limits. Provider Retry-After and longer persisted failure deadlines always win. The RSS refresh preference controls how often the open app checks eligibility; manual refresh cannot bypass source floors. SANS failure backoff starts at one hour and doubles up to 24 hours; other feed failure backoff starts at 15 minutes. Briefing freshness uses the larger of the configured refresh interval and each source floor, then a two-interval threshold.

## Evidence collection and limitations

- Primary publisher pages were read with browsing/search. Search-index text exposed CVE usage terms even though direct CVE terms rendering required JavaScript. No private policy or permission letter was obtained.
- One bounded, identified HTTPS read per robots URL returned 200 for SANS, Krebs and BleepingComputer; cveawg.mitre.org/robots.txt returned 404. The Krebs homepage was read to confirm its actual RSS alternate link and copyright attribution. No article bodies were collected into the database, and no feed refresh/ingest or personal-record mutation was performed for this review.
- Robots observations are dated snapshots, not a runtime crawler-policy engine. The app uses fixed feed paths, not site crawling, comments, login/search or publisher article fetching. It does not impersonate a permitted crawler or bypass challenges. Its existing GapSound user agent remains identifiable.
- Published policies may change. Recheck before changing endpoints, increasing request volume or moving beyond private local reading. No numeric quota found means unknown, not unlimited.

3. Verify notice retention and any additional rights for SANS diary text, BleepingComputer material and publisher-derived saved briefings before public/commercial use. Existing local history is preserved, not silently deleted.

Those restrictions do not authorize a public deployment or a publisher outreach message. Future changes must retain accurate provenance and respect any more restrictive provider response. Publisher permission questions are distinct from the already-implemented DNS, input validation, transactional import, backoff and retention controls.
